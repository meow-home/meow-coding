Todo popup — 4 directions

Four spatial answers to the same brief: keep the agent's todo observable without permanently eating chat space. Each mockup is live — click the trigger to expand/collapse, drag the progress slider to watch the state change. All four share the app's dark palette (--accent #007acc, --orange #ffb454, --green #4ade9f).

Idea. A small pill floats over the chat's top-right. The ring + 3/7 is the always-on glance; click to drop a compact card with the full list. It never pushes the feed — it overlays it. The dropdown is capped at 15rem and scrolls when the list is long. Best when you want zero layout shift.
