Meow pet — colourways

Same shape (variant B) and the same stylesheet the mockup uses, so every mood below is the real animation, not a still. Each card shows the colour on the two surfaces the mascot actually sits on — the composer card in the dark theme (#1f2123) and in the light theme (#ffffff) — at 72px, at the shipped 36px, and then every mood at 40px on the dark card.

A colourway is “safe” when its shell separates from both #1f2123 and #ffffff — a mid tone, or a two-tone body whose light and dark halves each do half the work. Eyes and mouth stay the logo's blue / orange wherever they can carry contrast; only the shell, rim, feet and patches move.
